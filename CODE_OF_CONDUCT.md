# Code of Conduct

This repository is intended to be a professional, respectful place to collaborate on public-interest software.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct
- Assume good intent, but discuss technical disagreements concretely
- Keep feedback focused on code, design, documentation, and observable behavior
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory or demeaning language
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or reviews
- Publishing private information without consent
- Any conduct that makes collaboration unsafe or unreasonably hostile

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project communication channels.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, report it to the maintainer by opening a private security-style report as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or by contacting the repository owner directly through GitHub.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or block participation from people who violate this policy.
